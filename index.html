Intermittent fasting window

  • 16:8 protocol. Eat between 12:00–20:00, fast 20:00–12:00.
  • Break the fast with protein (eggs, Greek yogurt, whey) to blunt hunger and protect muscle.
  • Train fasted or feed first? Lift fed (carbs before weights). Easy runs are fine fasted; hard intervals & Hyrox feed first.
  • Black coffee, tea, water, electrolytes are free during the fast.

The cheat day & drinking — read this

This is the single biggest lever on whether you hit 71 kg. One unmanaged cheat day can erase a week's deficit.

  • Cap it at one meal, not a whole day. "Cheat meal" beats "cheat day" 10:1.
  • Drinking twice a week is the main risk. Alcohol halts fat-burning while it's metabolised and crushes next-day training. Aim for one drinking occasion/week during these 6 weeks.
  • Smart drinks: spirits + soda/zero mixer, dry wine. Avoid beer & cocktails (liquid calories).
  • Protect the session before & after. Eat protein, hydrate, no fried late-night food.
  • Put cheat meal on a training day — extra carbs get used, not stored.
